Dear Ode Team,

Your role description reads like a job I have been doing, in pieces, for fifteen years: part engagement manager, part product manager, part delivery manager, part do-whatever-it-takes operator. My career is that hybrid in sequence — developer, then project manager owning customer relationships and contract negotiations, then product manager, then VP of Product & Engineering. Ode is Anthropic's services venture; I have spent most of my career at exactly that seam, where messy client problems have to become shipped, adopted systems.

The proof point: at TKXS, I walked into a revenue-led agriculture-data services company and led its transformation into a product enterprise — reframing fuzzy client work into a configurable portfolio, winning C-suite and Board commitment, running the weekly steering rhythm that kept delivery honest, and managing a $5.5M capital budget across a ~$40M portfolio. That is the forward-deployed motion at company scale: frame the problem, define success, ship, drive adoption, and hold the line on quality when stakeholders pull in different directions.

You ask for a willingness to overuse AI. Here is what that looks like in my actual week: I founded and lead Maverick's AI Center of Excellence — governance, practical use cases, art-of-the-possible demos. I have built six-plus working applications with Claude Code. I self-host LLM inference at home and run an open-source personal agent. And my job search itself runs on a multi-agent Claude pipeline — scheduled agents that scan, score, and verify roles daily and assemble tailored materials. This application was assembled inside that system. I do not need convincing that agents can do real work; I need harder problems to point them at.

A candid note on level: I have held the VP title, and it is not what I am optimizing for. What I want is what your posting describes — outsized autonomy, multiple products a year, and constant time on the frontier of models and tooling. The "biggest growth period of their career" line is the appeal, not the fine print. I am based in Raleigh-Durham, where Ode already has an office, and I am glad to talk through location fit, including SF or NY.

I would welcome the conversation.

Sincerely,
**Paul McClintock**
